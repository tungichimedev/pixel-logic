Full Description
4000 chars max
Pixel Logic is a nonogram puzzle game where you fill cells based on number clues to reveal hidden pixel art pictures. HOW TO PLAY Each puzzle is a grid with number clues on every row and column. The numbers tell you how many consecutive cells to fill. Use logic to figure out which cells to fill and which to leave empty. Complete the puzzle to reveal a pixel art picture! FEATURES ★ 30 Hand-Crafted Puzzles across 2 themed packs (Animals & Space) ★ Two Grid Sizes — 5×5 for beginners, 10×10 for a challenge ★ Daily Challenge — A new puzzle every day with bonus XP and hints ★ Pixel Art Reveals — Solve puzzles to uncover colorful pixel art ★ Progress System — Earn XP, level up, and unlock new puzzle packs ★ Star Ratings — Earn 1-3 stars based on speed and accuracy ★ Drag to Fill — Swipe across cells for fast filling ★ Fill & Mark Modes — Fill cells or mark them as empty ★ Hint System — Stuck? Use a hint to reveal a cell ★ Error Checking — Wrong cells flash red (disable with Pro) ★ Achievements — Complete milestones for bragging rights ★ Gallery — Build your pixel art collection ★ Beautiful Dark Theme — Easy on the eyes for late-night puzzling PERFECT FOR • Logic puzzle enthusiasts • Nonogram/Picross fans • Pixel art lovers • Relaxing brain training • Quick commute games or long focus sessions PIXEL LOGIC PRO Remove ads, unlock all packs, get weekly hints, and enable Purist Mode (no error checking) for the ultimate nonogram experience. New puzzles added regularly. Happy puzzling!
