Full Description

Pixel Logic is a nonogram and picross puzzle game — solve logic clues to reveal hidden pixel art! Fill cells on a grid using number clues in this relaxing brain game. Daily puzzles, 30 hand-crafted levels, and beautiful colored pixel art reveals. HOW TO PLAY Each puzzle is a grid with number clues on every row and column. The numbers tell you how many consecutive cells to fill. Use logic to figure out which cells to fill and which to leave empty. Complete the puzzle to reveal a colorful pixel art picture! FEATURES ★ 30 Hand-Crafted Puzzles across 2 themed packs (Animals & Space) ★ Two Grid Sizes — 5×5 for beginners, 10×10 for a challenge ★ Daily Challenge — A new puzzle every day with bonus XP and hints ★ Pixel Art Reveals — Solve puzzles to uncover colorful pixel art ★ Progress System — Earn XP, level up, and unlock new puzzle packs ★ Star Ratings — Earn 1-3 stars based on speed and accuracy ★ Drag to Fill — Swipe across cells for fast filling ★ Fill & Mark Modes — Fill cells or mark them as empty ★ Hint System — Stuck? Use a hint to reveal a cell ★ Error Checking — Wrong cells flash red (disable with Pro) ★ Achievements — Complete milestones for bragging rights ★ Gallery — Build your pixel art collection ★ Beautiful Dark Theme — Easy on the eyes for late-night puzzling WHY PIXEL LOGIC? Unlike other nonogram apps, every solved puzzle reveals a colorful pixel art picture — not just a black-and-white grid. Watch animals, rockets, and more come to life as you solve! PERFECT FOR • Nonogram and Picross fans who want beautiful reveals • Pixel art lovers and collectors • Relaxing brain training — no time pressure • Quick commute puzzles or long focus sessions • Anyone new to logic puzzles — easy tutorial included GO AD-FREE Remove ads for just $2.99 (one-time) and enjoy uninterrupted puzzling. Need a nudge? Grab a hint pack ($1.99). PIXEL LOGIC PRO ($1.99/month or $9.99/year) The ultimate experience — no ads, all packs unlocked, weekly hints, and Purist Mode (no error checking). One subscription, everything included. New puzzles added regularly. Happy puzzling!
