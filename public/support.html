Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play nonograms?

Tap cells to fill them based on the number clues on each row and column. The numbers tell you how many consecutive filled cells are in that line. Complete the grid to reveal a pixel art picture!

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  • iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Pixel Logic > Cancel
  • Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > Pixel Logic > Cancel

I lost my progress. Can I recover it?

Game progress is currently stored locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, progress may be lost. We're working on cloud sync for a future update.

How do I remove ads?

You can remove ads by purchasing the "Remove Ads" option ($2.99 one-time) or subscribing to Pixel Logic Pro ($1.99/month or $9.99/year).

The app is crashing or not working correctly.

Please try the following:

  • Make sure you have the latest version of the app
  • Restart the app
  • Restart your device
  • If the issue persists, email us with your device model and OS version
