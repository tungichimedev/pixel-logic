## The wedge (recent 1–2★)
- *"first few levels ad-free, then it randomly spikes to an unbearable number of ads… had to uninstall."*
- *"after every level an ad."*
- *"this is a kids game — health bar + auto-correction + auto-drawn lines kills the fun."*
- *"why does this simple puzzle make my phone overheat and crash?"*

## Positioning takeaways
1. **Cozy theme is the differentiator** (Meow Tower/Two Eyes prove it) — lean into the charming pixel-art reveal.
2. **Respect the player**: fair ad cadence + cheap remove-ads IAP + no overheating/crashes = better retention = more lifetime ad impressions.
3. Don't over-assist (no auto-correct that "kills the fun") — offer it as optional.
